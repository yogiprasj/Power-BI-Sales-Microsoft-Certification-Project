# Adventure Works Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive business intelligence dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales performance, product revenue, customer demographics, and geographic distribution using the Adventure Works dataset.

The project was completed as part of the **Microsoft Data Analysis and Visualization with Power BI** course on Coursera. The dataset was provided as a learning resource within the course, while the dashboard design, data modeling, visualizations, and analytical findings were developed through hands-on project activities.

The dashboard consists of two analytical pages: **Sales Analytics** and **Customer Analytics**. Together, they provide an overview of business performance, product-level revenue contributions, payment method distribution, and customer characteristics.

This project demonstrates practical skills in data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboard development, and business data analysis.

---

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate overall sales performance using key business metrics.
- Analyze revenue trends and identify leading product categories, subcategories, and individual products.
- Examine payment method distribution and sales patterns.
- Understand customer demographics and geographic distribution.
- Analyze customer-level revenue contributions and purchasing behavior across age groups.
- Present business data through interactive visualizations to support data-driven analysis.

## Dataset

This project uses the **Adventure Works dataset**, provided as a learning dataset within the Microsoft Data Analysis and Visualization with Power BI course on Coursera.

## Key Findings

### Sales Performance

- The dashboard records **$147.80K in total revenue** from **48 orders** and **78 units sold**.
- The average order value is approximately **$3.08K**.
- Revenue fluctuates over the observed period, with variations across order dates.

### Product Performance

- **Mountain Bikes** and **Road Bikes** are the leading product categories by revenue.
- **Downhill** ranks among the leading product subcategories by revenue.
- **AeroSpeed 1000** is among the top-performing products by revenue.

### Payment Method Analysis

- **Credit Card** accounts for **60.42%** of recorded orders.
- **PayPal** accounts for the remaining **39.58%**.

Credit Card is the more frequently used payment method in the dataset.

### Customer Analysis

- The dashboard covers **48 customers** with an average age of **35 years**.
- Customers are distributed across **18 cities** and **15 countries**.
- **Europe** represents the largest customer group by continent, accounting for **41.67%** of customers.
- The **United States** has the highest customer count among individual countries, with **8 customers**.

These findings provide an overview of product revenue contributions, payment preferences, and customer distribution within the analyzed dataset.

## Business Recommendations

### Prioritize Leading Product Categories

Use revenue performance across product categories and subcategories to inform product prioritization, promotional planning, and inventory discussions.

### Evaluate Payment Method Preferences

Consider the predominance of Credit Card payments when reviewing payment options and customer checkout preferences.

### Apply Customer Segmentation

Use customer age and geographic distribution to develop more focused customer segments for marketing and sales analysis.

### Monitor Customer Revenue Contributions

Review customer-level revenue contributions to identify high-value customers and inform further analysis of purchasing behavior.

*These recommendations are based on the patterns visible in the dashboard. Additional transaction history and customer-level data would be needed to validate retention, repeat-purchase behavior, and customer lifetime value.*
